# Data licence

## The catalogue

The Submersion site catalogue (`site_catalogue.json` and
`site_catalogue_open.json` in each release, and the researched and curated
sites in this repository) is made available under the
[Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

It contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available under the
Open Database License: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors,
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Every source, its licence and its credit are listed in the catalogue file's own
notice (`metadata.notice` and `metadata.sources`).

## Sources on their own terms

Four sources allow reuse only on their own terms. Their records ship in
`site_catalogue.json`, marked with their source, but they are **not part of
the ODbL database** and are left out of `site_catalogue_open.json`:

| Source | Terms |
|---|---|
| New Jersey reef deployments (NJDEP) | Redistributed only with NJDEP's metadata, credit and disclaimer, coordinates as received |
| North Carolina Artificial Reef Guide | Non-commercial use, unaltered, citing the N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries |
| Texas artificial reefs | Non-commercial use with credit: "Information courtesy Texas Parks and Wildlife Department © 2022"; not to be sold |
| Hawaii artificial reefs | Not for commercial purposes |

The same applies to their raw snapshots in the `snapshots-YYYY-MM-DD`
releases: each file's `TERMS.txt` states its terms.

## Not for navigation

Positions are where published sources put a site, often a mooring or an entry
point. They are not for navigation.
