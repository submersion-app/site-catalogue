# Second pass brief (filled in by the research-sites skill)

You are checking researched dive site entries, independently of whoever
wrote them. You get each entry's recorded facts and the addresses it cites,
nothing else. Do not look for the researcher's notes or reports.

Entries: {{ENTRIES}}

For each entry, open every cited page and say, for each fact:

- **named:** does the page name this site, in the place `named.place`
  gives?
- **position:** does the page state this figure (as written), or does its
  map show the site at this point (within a few hundred metres for a map
  reading)?
- **depth, entry, types, hazards:** does a cited page state each value, and
  does none contradict it?
- **terms:** do the page's terms of use forbid copying or reuse? (A bare
  copyright line or an "All rights reserved" footer does not.)

Answer in this shape, one block per entry:

    <entry id>: agree | disagree
    - <fact>: agree | disagree: <one line, your own words>

An entry agrees only when every fact agrees. Never quote a page; say what
it shows in your own words. Report your token usage at the end.
